He aha ka Mill (Nine Men's Morris)?

ʻO ka Mill (Nine Men's Morris) kekahi o nā pāʻani papa kahiko loa o ka honua, i pāʻani ʻia no nā makahiki ʻoi aku ma mua o 2,000. Hoʻokaʻawale nā mea pāʻani ʻelua i kekahi papa me 24 kiko, i hoʻopili ʻia e nā laina. He ʻeiwa ʻāpana ko kēlā me kēia mea pāʻani — ʻeleʻele kekahi ʻaoʻao, keʻokeʻo kekahi — a he maʻalahi ka pahuhopu: e hoʻolālani i ʻekolu o kāu mau ʻāpana e lawe ai i nā ʻāpana o kou hoa paio, a kokoke pau nā mea a kou hoa paio.

ʻElua māhele o ka pāʻani. ʻO ka mua, e kau ʻoe i kāu mau ʻāpana, pakahi, ma nā kiko hakahaka. A laila, ke kau ʻia nā ʻāpana a pau ma ka papa, e hoʻoheʻe ʻoe iā lākou ma nā laina i nā kiko hakahaka e pili ana. He maʻalahi ke kani ʻana, akā he mea nui kēlā me kēia kau ʻana: hiki ke paʻakikī ke pale aku i kekahi ʻāpana i kau mālama ʻole ʻia i ka hoʻomaka ʻana ma hope.

Ma Pare Asobi, hiki iā ʻoe ke pāʻani i ka Mill me ka manuahi ma kāu polokalamu kele pūnaewele, ma kāu kelepona a ma kāu kamepiula paha. ʻAʻohe mea e hoʻoiho ai a ʻaʻohe moʻokāki e hana ai — e wehe wale i ka pāʻani a hoʻomaka e pāʻani.

Pehea e pāʻani ai

  1. ʻO ka mua, e kau i kāu mau ʻāpana. E hoʻololi i ka kau ʻana i kekahi ʻāpana ma kekahi kiko hakahaka. ʻEleʻele ka mea e neʻe mua. E hoʻomau a kau ʻia nā ʻāpana ʻeiwa o kēlā me kēia ʻaoʻao ma ka papa.
  2. E hana i mill e lawe ai i kekahi ʻāpana. Ke hoʻolālani ʻia ʻekolu o kāu mau ʻāpana ma kekahi laina i hoʻopili ʻia, ua hana ʻoe i mill, a hiki iā ʻoe ke wehe i kekahi ʻāpana o kou hoa paio mai ka papa. ʻAʻole hiki iā ʻoe ke lawe i kekahi ʻāpana i loko o ka mill — ke ʻole nā ʻāpana a pau o kou hoa paio i loko o ka mill.
  3. A laila, e hoʻoneʻe i kāu mau ʻāpana. Ke kau ʻia nā mea a pau, e hoʻoheʻe i kekahi ʻāpana i ka manawa i kekahi kiko hakahaka e pili ana ma kekahi laina. Hiki i ka mill i hana ʻia ma ka neʻe ʻana ke ʻae iā ʻoe e lawe i kekahi ʻāpana o kou hoa paio.
  4. Koe wale nō ʻekolu ʻāpana iā ʻoe? Hiki iā ʻoe ke lele. Ke koe wale nō ʻekolu ʻāpana iā ʻoe, ʻaʻole ʻoe i kaupalena ʻia i nā kiko e pili ana — hiki iā ʻoe ke lele i kekahi kiko hakahaka ma ka papa.
  5. Pehea e lanakila ai. Lanakila ʻoe ma ka hoʻemi ʻana i kou hoa paio i ʻelua ʻāpana, a ma ka pale ʻana iā ia i ʻole e koe kekahi neʻe. Inā hana hou ʻia ke kūlana hoʻokahi ʻekolu manawa, a inā ʻaʻohe mill i hana ʻia no 50 neʻe, pau ka pāʻani ma ke kaha ʻakomi.

Nā mana ʻelua: ka Mill kahiko a me ka Morabaraba

Ka Mill kahiko (Nine Men's Morris) pāʻani ʻia me ʻeiwa ʻāpana no kēlā me kēia ʻaoʻao, ma ka papa 24 kiko i wehewehe ʻia ma luna.

ʻO ka Morabaraba ka mana 12 ʻāpana o ka pāʻani. He ʻumikūmālua ʻāpana ko kēlā me kēia mea pāʻani, a loaʻa i ka papa ʻehā laina hou ma ka ʻaoʻao ʻākau. Hiki i nā ʻāpana ke neʻe ma ka ʻaoʻao ʻākau kekahi, a hana pū ka ʻekolu ma ka lālani ma ka ʻaoʻao ʻākau i mill. Pāʻani wikiwiki a wehe ʻia ma mua o ka pāʻani kahiko, a uku ʻia nā maka ʻoi no nā laina ma ka ʻaoʻao ʻākau.

Loaʻa nā mana ʻelua i ka pāʻani hoʻokahi. Hiki iā ʻoe ke hoʻololi ma waena o lākou i kēlā me kēia manawa āu e hoʻomaka ai i kekahi pāʻani hou.

Nā ala e pāʻani ai

E pāʻani ma ka pūnaewele me nā kānaka a puni ka honua. E hoʻomaka i kekahi pāʻani wikiwiki a e hālāwai me kekahi hoa paio hou i kēia manawa. He helu, kūlana, a me nā mea nānā ko ka pāʻani pūnaewele, me ʻekolu wikiwiki: maʻamau (30 kekona no kēlā me kēia neʻe), lohi (2 minuke no kēlā me kēia neʻe) a me ka leka (24 hola no kēlā me kēia neʻe).

E pāʻani me ke kamepiula. ʻEkolu pae kamepiula — maʻalahi, maʻamau, a ikaika — i hiki iā ʻoe ke aʻo i nā lula ma kāu wikiwiki ponoʻī a laila e hakakā me kekahi hoa paio ikaika.

E pāʻani me ʻelua mea pāʻani ma kekahi mea hoʻokahi. E kaʻana like i kekahi kelepona a i kekahi pale me kekahi hoaaloha a e hoʻololi i ka kau ʻana a me ka hoʻoneʻe ʻana i nā ʻāpana, e like me ka papa maoli.

Loaʻa ka pāʻani ma nā ʻōlelo 100. ʻŌlelo ka pāʻani i kāu ʻōlelo, a hiki iā ʻoe ke hoʻololi iā ia mai nā hoʻonohonoho i kēlā me kēia manawa.

Loaʻa ka pāʻani pūnaewele i kēia manawa

I kēia manawa, hiki iā ʻoe ke pāʻani ma ka pūnaewele me nā kānaka a puni ka honua. E hoʻomaka i kekahi pāʻani wikiwiki a e hālāwai me kekahi hoa paio hou i kēia manawa.

He ʻekolu wikiwiki ko ka pāʻani pūnaewele: maʻamau (30 kekona no kēlā me kēia neʻe), lohi (2 minuke no kēlā me kēia neʻe) a me ka leka (24 hola no kēlā me kēia neʻe), me nā helu, nā kūlana, a me nā mea nānā. Hiki iā ʻoe ke pāʻani me ke kamepiula a me kekahi hoaaloha ma ka mea hoʻokahi kekahi.

Nā nīnau i nīnau pinepine ʻia

He manuahi anei ka Mill (Nine Men's Morris)? ʻAe. He manuahi loa ka Mill ma Pare Asobi e pāʻani ai, ʻaʻohe kau inoa a ʻaʻohe polokalamu e hoʻouka ai.

Hiki iaʻu ke pāʻani ma kaʻu kelepona? ʻAe. Hana ka pāʻani ma nā kelepona, nā papa, a me nā kamepiula, pololei ma kāu polokalamu kele pūnaewele.

He aha ka mill? ʻO ka mill ʻekolu o kāu mau ʻāpana ma ka lālani ma kekahi laina i hoʻopili ʻia. Hiki i ka hana ʻana i mill ke ʻae iā ʻoe e wehe i kekahi ʻāpana o kou hoa paio mai ka papa.

He aha ka Morabaraba? ʻO ka Morabaraba ka mana 12 ʻāpana o ka Mill, me ʻehā laina hou ma ka ʻaoʻao ʻākau ma ka papa. ʻAe ʻia nā neʻe ma ka ʻaoʻao ʻākau a me nā mill ma ka ʻaoʻao ʻākau.

Ke ʻimi nei ʻoe i nā pāʻani hou aʻe? E ʻike i nā pāʻani manuahi ʻeono a pau ma ka ʻaoʻao mua o Pare Asobi ma ka ʻōlelo Hawaiʻi, me ka Reversi (Othello), ka Sudoku (Nanpure), ka Crossword, ka Shirupure, a me ka Wado Kaichin.
