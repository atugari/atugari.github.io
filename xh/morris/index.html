Yintoni iMill (Nine Men's Morris)?

IMill (Nine Men's Morris) ngomnye wemidlalo yebhodi emidala kwihlabathi, udlalwa ngaphezulu kweminyaka engama-2,000. Abadlali ababini babelana ngebhodi enye enamanqaku angama-24, adityaniswe yimigca. Umdlali ngamnye unamaqhekeza alithoba — icala elinye limnyama, elinye limhlophe — kwaye injongo ilula: beka amaqhekeza akho amathathu emgceni ukuze uthathe amaqhekeza omchasi wakho, de abe nomchasi wakho usele phantse nanto.

Umdlalo unamacandelo amabini. Kuqala ubeka amaqhekeza akho, elinye ngexesha, kumanqaku angenanto. Emva koko, xa onke amaqhekeza esebhodini, uwatyibilikisa ecaleni kwimigca ukuya kumanqaku angenanto akufutshane. Kuvakala kulula, kodwa ukubeka ngakunye kubalulekile: iqhekeza elibekwe ngokungakhathali ekuqaleni kunokuba nzima ukulikhusela emva koko.

E-Pare Asobi ungadlala iMill simahla kwibhrawuza yakho, kwifowuni yakho okanye kwikhompyutha yakho. Akukho nto yokukhuphela kwaye akukho akhawunti yokudala — vula nje umdlalo uze uqale ukudlala.

Indlela yokudlala

  1. Kuquka, beka amaqhekeza akho. Tshintshana ngokubeka iqhekeza elinye kwindawo engenanto. UMnyama uhamba kuqala. Qhubeka de amaqhekeza alithoba ecala ngalinye abekho ebhodini.
  2. Yenza imill ukuze uthathe iqhekeza. Xa amaqhekeza akho amathathu emgceni kumgca odibeneyo, wenze imill, kwaye unokususa elinye lamaqhekeza omchasi wakho ebhodini. Awukwazi ukuthatha iqhekeza elikwimill — ngaphandle kokuba onke amaqhekeza omchasi wakho ekwimill.
  3. Emva koko hambisa amaqhekeza akho. Xa yonke into ibekiwe, tyibilikisa iqhekeza elinye ngexesha ukuya kwindawo engenanto ekufutshane ecaleni komgca. Imill eyenziwe ngokuhamba nayo ikuvumela ukuba uthathe elinye lamaqhekeza omchasi wakho.
  4. Usele namaqhekeza amathathu? Ungabhabha. Xa usele namaqhekeza amathathu kuphela, awusakhawulelwanga kumanqaku akufutshane — ungabhabhela kuyo nayiphi na indawo engenanto ebhodini.
  5. Indlela yokuphumelela. Uphumelela ngokunciphisa umchasi wakho abe namaqhekeza amabini, okanye ngokumthintela ukuze angabi nayo intshukumo eshiyekileyo. Ukuba indawo efanayo iphinda kabini kathathu, okanye ukuba akukho mill yenziweyo kwiintshukumo ezingama-50, umdlalo uphela ngokulingana ngokuzenzekelayo.

Iinguqulelo ezimbini: iMill yakudala kunye neMorabaraba

IMill yakudala (Nine Men's Morris) idlalwa ngamaqhekeza alithoba kwicala ngalinye, kwibhodi enamanqaku angama-24 echazwe ngasentla.

IMorabaraba yinguqulelo yamaqhekeza ali-12 yomdlalo. Umdlali ngamnye unamaqhekeza alishumi elinambini, kwaye ibhodi ifumana imigca emine eyongezelelweyo ethe tyaba. Amaqhekeza nawo angahamba ethe tyaba, kwaye amathathu emgceni ethe tyaba nawo enza imill. Idlala ngokukhawuleza nangokuvulelekileyo kunomdlalo wakudala, kwaye ivuza amehlo abukhali kwimigca ethe tyaba.

Zombini iinguqulelo zifumaneka kumdlalo omnye. Ungatshintsha phakathi kwazo nanini na xa uqala umdlalo omtsha.

Iindlela zokudlala

Dlala kwi-intanethi nabantu kwihlabathi jikelele. Qala umdlalo okhawulezayo uze udibane nomchasi omtsha kwangoko. Umdlalo we-intanethi unomlinganiselo, isikhundla kunye nababukeli, ngesantya ezithathu: esiqhelekileyo (imizuzwana engama-30 ngentshukumo nganye), esicothayo (imizuzu emibini ngentshukumo nganye) kunye nelileta (iiyure ezingama-24 ngentshukumo nganye).

Dlala nekhompyutha. Amanqanaba ekhompyutha amathathu akhona — elula, eliqhelekileyo, kunye nelinzima — ukuze ufunde imithetho ngesantya sakho uze ujongane nomchasi onzima.

Dlala nabadlali ababini kwisixhobo esinye. Yabelana ngefowuni enye okanye isikrini esinye nomhlobo uze utshintshane ngokubeka nokuhambisa amaqhekeza, njengoko kwibhodi yokwenyani.

Lo mdlalo uyafumaneka ngeelwimi ezili-100. Umdlalo uthetha ulwimi lwakho, kwaye unokulutshintsha useto nangaliphi na ixesha.

Umdlalo we-intanethi uyafumaneka ngoku

Ngoku ungadlala kwi-intanethi nabantu kwihlabathi jikelele. Qala umdlalo okhawulezayo uze udibane nomchasi omtsha kwangoko.

Umdlalo we-intanethi unesantya ezithathu: esiqhelekileyo (imizuzwana engama-30 ngentshukumo nganye), esicothayo (imizuzu emibini ngentshukumo nganye) kunye nelileta (iiyure ezingama-24 ngentshukumo nganye), kunye nomlinganiselo, isikhundla kunye nababukeli. Ungadlala nekhompyutha okanye nomhlobo kwisixhobo esinye.

Imibuzo ebuzwa rhoqo

Ingaba iMill (Nine Men's Morris) isimahla? Ewe. IMill ePare Asobi isimahla ngokupheleleyo ukudlala, akukho kubhalisa kwaye akukho usetyenziso lokufakela.

Ndingadlala kwifowuni yam? Ewe. Umdlalo usebenza kwiifowuni, kwiitafileti nakwiikhompyutha, ngqo kwibhrawuza yakho.

Yintoni imill? Imill ngamaqhekeza akho amathathu emgceni kumgca odibeneyo. Ukwenza imill kukuvumela ukuba ususe elinye lamaqhekeza omchasi wakho ebhodini.

Yintoni iMorabaraba? IMorabaraba yinguqulelo yamaqhekeza ali-12 yeMill, enemigca emine eyongezelelweyo ethe tyaba ebhodini. Iintshukumo ezithe tyaba kunye neemill ezithe tyaba zivumelekile.

Ufuna eminye imidlalo? Jonga yonke imidlalo emithandathu yasimahla kwi iphepha eliphambili lePare Asobi ngesiXhosa, kuquka iReversi (Othello), iSudoku (Nanpure), iCrossword, iShirupure kunye neWado Kaichin.
