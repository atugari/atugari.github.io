Kodi Reversi (Othello) ndi chiyani?

Reversi, yomwe imadziwikanso kuti Othello, ndi masewera a board akale a osewera awiri. Wosewera mmodzi amatenga ma disiki akuda, wina amatenga ma disiki oyera, ndipo mbali zonse ziwiri zimagawana board imodzi. Malamulo amatenga pafupifupi mphindi imodzi kuti aphunzire, koma kusuntha kumodzi kungathe kusintha masewera onse — ndichomwe chimapangitsa Reversi kukhala yovuta kuisiya.

Board wamba ndi 8 ndi 8 mabwalo, ndipo masewerawa amayamba ndi ma disiki anayi pakati: akuda awiri ndi oyera awiri, oikidwa m'mbali. Kuchokera pamenepo, osewera amasinthana kuyika disiki imodzi panthawi, ndipo disiki iliyonse yomwe amatchera imasanduka mtundu wawo. Board ikadzadza, kapena ngati palibe wosewera amene ali ndi kusuntha kovomerezeka kotsala, masewerawa amatha ndipo wosewera wokhala ndi ma disiki ambiri pa board amapambana.

Pa Pare Asobi mutha kusewera Reversi kwaulere mu msakatuli wanu, pa foni yanu kapena pa kompyuta yanu. Palibe choti mutsitse ndipo palibe akaunti yoti mupange — ingotsegulani masewerawa ndipo yambani kusewera.

Momwe mungasewere

  1. Wakuda amasuntha choyamba. Ikani disiki pa bwalo lopanda kanthu kuti itchere disiki imodzi kapena zambiri za mdani wanu mu mzere wowongoka — kudutsa, pansi, kapena m'mbali — pakati pa disiki yanu yatsopano ndi disiki ya mtundu wanu yomwe ili kale pa board.
  2. Disiki iliyonse yotcheredwa imasanduka mtundu wanu. Kusuntha kumodzi kungathe kutembenuza mzere wonse wa ma disiki nthawi imodzi, kotero kusuntha kumodzi kwabwino kungathe kusintha zigoli kwambiri.
  3. Ngati mulibe kusuntha kovomerezeka, mumadutsa. Mdani wanu amasunthanso. Masewerawa amapitirira mpaka board itadzadza kapena palibe wosewera amene angasunthe.
  4. Wosewera wokhala ndi ma disiki ambiri amapambana. Zigoli zomaliza ndi chiwerengero chabe cha ma disiki a mtundu uliwonse otsala pa board.
  5. Makona ndi mabwalo otetezeka kwambiri. Mukatenga kona, siyingatembenuzidwe. Mabwalo omwe ali pafupi ndi kona ndi oopsa, chifukwa angathe kupereka kona imeneyo kwa mdani wanu.

Njira zosewerera

Sewerani pa intaneti ndi anthu padziko lonse lapansi. Yambani masewera ofulumira ndipo kumanani ndi mdani watsopano nthawi yomweyo. Masewera okhala ndi mavoti amagwiritsa ntchito mavoti, maudindo, mindandanda ya maudindo ndi mitu, kuti muwone voti yanu ikukwera pamene mukupambana. Masewera opumula alibe mavoti ndipo ndi ochedwa, kuti musangalale ndi masewerawa popanda kudandaula za voti yanu.

Sewerani ndi kompyuta. Pali magawo asanu a kompyuta, kuyambira osavuta mpaka wamphamvu kwambiri. Yambani pa gawo osavuta kuti muphunzire malamulo, kenako kwelani gawo limodzi panthawi mpaka gawo lamphamvu kwambiri likupatseni vuto lenileni.

Sewerani ndi osewera awiri pa chipangizo chimodzi. Gawanani foni imodzi kapena sikirini imodzi ndi mnzanu ndipo sinthani kuyika ma disiki, monga board yeniyeni.

Yesani ma board okhala ndi mawonekedwe osazolowereka. Mu masewera a kompyuta ndi masewera a osewera awiri pa chipangizo chimodzi, mutha kusankha mawonekedwe a board: board wamba wa 8 ndi 8, wotambasuka, mtima, donati, dayimondi, kapena mtanda. Mawonekedwe aliwonse amasintha chiyambi ndi momwe masewerawa amachitikira.

Mofulumira kapena mochedwa — chisankho chanu. Masewera wamba amakupatsani masekondi 20 pa kusuntha kulikonse kuti musewere mofulumira komanso mwachangu. Ngati mukufuna kuganiza mosamala, masewera opumula amakupatsani nthawi yochuluka. Masewerawa akupezeka m'zinenero 100.

Mafunso omwe amafunsidwa kawirikawiri

Kodi Reversi ndi yaulere? Inde. Reversi pa Pare Asobi ndi yaulere kwathunthu kusewera, palibe kulembetsa ndipo palibe pulogalamu yoti muyike.

Kodi ndingasewere pa foni yanga? Inde. Masewerawa amagwira ntchito pa mafoni, matableti, ndi makompyuta, mwachindunji mu msakatuli wanu.

Kodi ndikufunika akaunti kuti ndisewe pa intaneti? Ayi. Mutha kuyamba masewera ofulumira pa intaneti popanda kupanga akaunti.

Kodi pali kusiyana kotani pakati pa Reversi ndi Othello? Ndi masewera omwewo. Othello ndi dzina lodziwika kwambiri la Reversi, ndipo mayina onse awiri amagwiritsidwa ntchito pa masewera otembenuza ma disiki akuda ndi oyera.

Mukufuna masewera ena? Onani masewera onse asanu ndi limodzi aulere pa tsamba loyamba la Pare Asobi m'Chichewa, kuphatikiza Sudoku (Nanpure), Mill (Nine Men's Morris), Crossword, Shirupure ndi Wado Kaichin.
