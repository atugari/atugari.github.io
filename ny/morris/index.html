Kodi Mill (Nine Men's Morris) ndi chiyani?

Mill (Nine Men's Morris) ndi umodzi mwamasewera a board akale kwambiri padziko lapansi, womwe wakhala ukuseweredwa kwa zaka zoposa 2,000. Osewera awiri amagawana board imodzi yokhala ndi malo 24, olumikizidwa ndi mizere. Wosewera aliyense ali ndi zidutswa zisanu ndi zinayi — mbali imodzi yakuda, ina yoyera — ndipo cholinga ndi chosavuta: konzani zidutswa zanu zitatu mzere kuti mutenge zidutswa za mdani wanu, mpaka mdani wanu atsale wopanda kanthu.

Masewerawa ali ndi magawo awiri. Choyamba mumayika zidutswa zanu, chimodzi panthawi, pamalo opanda kanthu. Kenako, zidutswa zonse zikakhala pa board, mumazisuntha motsatira mizere kupita kumalo opanda kanthu apafupi. Zimamveka zosavuta, koma kuyika kulikonse ndikofunika: chidutswa chomwe chinayikidwa mosasamala pachiyambi chingakhale chovuta kuchiteteza pambuyo pake.

Pa Pare Asobi mutha kusewera Mill kwaulere mu msakatuli wanu, pa foni yanu kapena pa kompyuta yanu. Palibe choti mutsitse ndipo palibe akaunti yoti mupange — ingotsegulani masewerawa ndipo yambani kusewera.

Momwe mungasewere

  1. Choyamba, ikani zidutswa zanu. Sinthani kuyika chidutswa chimodzi pamalo opanda kanthu. Wakuda amasuntha choyamba. Pitirizani mpaka zidutswa zisanu ndi zinayi za mbali iliyonse zikhale pa board.
  2. Pangani mill kuti mutenge chidutswa. Zidutswa zanu zitatu zikakhala mzere pa mzere wolumikizidwa, mwapanga mill, ndipo mutha kuchotsa chidutswa chimodzi cha mdani wanu pa board. Simungatenge chidutswa chomwe chili mu mill — pokhapokha zidutswa zonse za mdani wanu zili mu mill.
  3. Kenako sunthani zidutswa zanu. Chilichonse chikayikidwa, sunthani chidutswa chimodzi panthawi kupita kumalo opanda kanthu apafupi motsatira mzere. Mill yomwe yapangidwa posuntha imalolanso kuti mutenge chidutswa chimodzi cha mdani wanu.
  4. Mwatsala ndi zidutswa zitatu? Mutha kuwuluka. Mukatsala ndi zidutswa zitatu zokha, simunamangidwenso ndi malo apafupi — mutha kuwulukira kumalo aliwonse opanda kanthu pa board.
  5. Momwe mungapambanire. Mumapambana pochepetsa mdani wanu kufika ku zidutswa ziwiri, kapena pomutsekera kuti asakhale ndi kusuntha kulikonse kotsala. Ngati malo omwewo abwerezedwa katatu, kapena ngati palibe mill yomwe yapangidwa kwa masuntho 50, masewerawa amatha ndi tayi yokha.

Mitundu iwiri: Mill yakale ndi Morabaraba

Mill yakale (Nine Men's Morris) imaseweredwa ndi zidutswa zisanu ndi zinayi mbali iliyonse, pa board ya malo 24 yomwe yafotokozedwa pamwambapa.

Morabaraba ndi mtundu wa zidutswa 12 wa masewerawa. Wosewera aliyense ali ndi zidutswa khumi ndi ziwiri, ndipo board imapeza mizere inayi yowonjezera ya m'mbali. Zidutswa zimatha kusunthanso m'mbali, ndipo zitatu mzere m'mbali zimapanganso mill. Imasewera mofulumira komanso motseguka kuposa masewera akale, ndipo imapereka mphotho kwa maso akuthwa kwa mizere ya m'mbali.

Mitundu yonse iwiri ikupezeka mu masewera omwewo. Mutha kusintha pakati pawo nthawi iliyonse mukayamba masewera atsopano.

Njira zosewerera

Sewerani pa intaneti ndi anthu padziko lonse lapansi. Yambani masewera ofulumira ndipo kumanani ndi mdani watsopano nthawi yomweyo. Masewera a pa intaneti ali ndi mavoti, maudindo ndi owonera, okhala ndi liwiro atatu: wamba (masekondi 30 pa kusuntha kulikonse), wochedwa (mphindi 2 pa kusuntha kulikonse) ndi wa kalata (maola 24 pa kusuntha kulikonse).

Sewerani ndi kompyuta. Pali magawo atatu a kompyuta — osavuta, wamba, ndi wamphamvu — kuti muphunzire malamulo pa liwiro lanu ndipo kenako mukumane ndi mdani wamphamvu.

Sewerani ndi osewera awiri pa chipangizo chimodzi. Gawanani foni imodzi kapena sikirini imodzi ndi mnzanu ndipo sinthani kuyika ndi kusuntha zidutswa, monga board yeniyeni.

Masewerawa akupezeka m'zinenero 100. Masewerawa amalankhula chinenero chanu, ndipo mungachisinthire kuchokera ku zoikamo nthawi iliyonse.

Masewera a pa intaneti akupezeka tsopano

Tsopano mutha kusewera pa intaneti ndi anthu padziko lonse lapansi. Yambani masewera ofulumira ndipo kumanani ndi mdani watsopano nthawi yomweyo.

Masewera a pa intaneti ali ndi liwiro atatu: wamba (masekondi 30 pa kusuntha kulikonse), wochedwa (mphindi 2 pa kusuntha kulikonse) ndi wa kalata (maola 24 pa kusuntha kulikonse), okhala ndi mavoti, maudindo ndi owonera. Muthanso kusewera ndi kompyuta kapena ndi mnzanu pa chipangizo chimodzi.

Mafunso omwe amafunsidwa kawirikawiri

Kodi Mill (Nine Men's Morris) ndi yaulere? Inde. Mill pa Pare Asobi ndi yaulere kwathunthu kusewera, palibe kulembetsa ndipo palibe pulogalamu yoti muyike.

Kodi ndingasewere pa foni yanga? Inde. Masewerawa amagwira ntchito pa mafoni, matableti, ndi makompyuta, mwachindunji mu msakatuli wanu.

Kodi mill ndi chiyani? Mill ndi zidutswa zanu zitatu mzere pa mzere wolumikizidwa. Kupanga mill kumakulolani kuti muchotse chidutswa chimodzi cha mdani wanu pa board.

Kodi Morabaraba ndi chiyani? Morabaraba ndi mtundu wa zidutswa 12 wa Mill, wokhala ndi mizere inayi yowonjezera ya m'mbali pa board. Kusuntha kwa m'mbali ndi mill za m'mbali ndizololedwa.

Mukufuna masewera ena? Onani masewera onse asanu ndi limodzi aulere pa tsamba loyamba la Pare Asobi m'Chichewa, kuphatikiza Reversi (Othello), Sudoku (Nanpure), Crossword, Shirupure ndi Wado Kaichin.
