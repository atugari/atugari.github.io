Chii chinonzi Reversi (Othello)?

Reversi, inozivikanwawo seOthello, mutambo webhodhi wekare wevatambi vaviri. Mumwe mutambi anotora madhisiki matema, mumwe anotora madhisiki machena, uye mativi ose anogoverana bhodhi rimwe. Mitemo inotora maminitsi anenge rimwe kudzidza, asi danho rimwe chete rinogona kushandura mutambo wese — ndicho chinoita kuti Reversi ive yakaoma kuisa pasi.

Bhodhi yakajairika ine 8 ne 8 mativi, uye mutambo unotanga nemadhisiki mana pakati: matema maviri nemachena maviri, akaiswa zvakatenuka. Kubva ipapo, vatambi vanochinjana kuisa dhisiki rimwe panguva, uye dhisiki rose ravanoteya rinopinduka kuva ruvara rwavo. Kana bhodhi yazara, kana kana pasina mutambi ane danho repamutemo rasara, mutambo unopera uye mutambi ane madhisiki akawanda pabhodhi anokunda.

PaPare Asobi unogona kutamba Reversi mahara mubrowser yako, pafoni yako kana pakombiyuta yako. Hapana chekudhawunirodha uye hapana akaundi yekugadzira — ingovhura mutambo wotanga kutamba.

Matambiro

  1. Dema rinofamba kutanga. Isa dhisiki pachivakwa chisina chinhu kuti iteye rimwe kana akawanda emadhisiki emuvengi wako mumutsara wakatwasuka — kuyambuka, pasi, kana zvakatenuka — pakati pedhisiki rako idzva nedhisiki reruvara rwako ratova pabhodhi.
  2. Dhisiki rose rakateyiwa rinopinduka kuva ruvara rwako. Danho rimwe chete rinogona kupindura mutsara wese wemadhisiki panguva imwe, saka danho rimwe rakanaka rinogona kuchinja zvibodzwa zvakawanda.
  3. Kana usina danho repamutemo, unopfuura. Muvengi wako anofamba zvakare. Mutambo unoenderera kusvikira bhodhi yazara kana pasina mutambi anogona kufamba.
  4. Mutambi ane madhisiki akawanda anokunda. Zvibodzwa zvekupedzisira ingori huwandu hwemadhisiki eruvara rimwe nerimwe akasara pabhodhi.
  5. Makona ndiwo mativi akachengeteka zvikuru. Kana watora kona, haichazopindurwi. Mativi ari pedyo nekona ane njodzi, nekuti anogona kupa kona iyoyo kumuvengi wako.

Nzira dzekutamba

Tamba online nevanhu pasi rose. Tanga mutambo unokurumidza usangane nemuvengi mutsva ipapo. Mitambo ine chiyero inoshandisa chiyero, zvinzvimbo, mazita ezvinzvimbo nemazita, kuti uone chiyero chako chichikwira paunokunda. Mitambo yekuzorora haina chiyero uye inononoka, kuti unakirwe nemutambo usinganetseki nechiyero chako.

Tamba nekomputa. Pane nhanho shanu dzekomputa, kubva payakapfava kusvika payakaoma. Tanga panhanho yakapfava kuti udzidze mitemo, wozokwira nhanho imwe panguva kusvikira nhanho yakaoma yakupa dambudziko chairo.

Tamba nevatambi vaviri pamudziyo mumwe. Goverana foni imwe kana skrini imwe neshamwari muchinjana kuisa madhisiki, sebhodhi chairo.

Edza mabhodhi ane maumbirwo asina kujairika. Mumitambo yekomputa nemitambo yevatambi vaviri pamudziyo mumwe, unogona kusarudza chimiro chebhodhi: bhodhi yakajairika ye 8 ne 8, yakafara, moyo, donati, dhaimani, kana muchinjikwa. Chimiro chimwe nechimwe chinoshandura kutanga uye mafambiro emutambo.

Inokurumidza kana inononoka — sarudzo yako. Mitambo yakajairika inokupa masekonzi 20 padanho rimwe kuti utambe nekukurumidza uye uine simba. Kana uchida kufunga zvakanaka, mutambo wekuzorora unokupa nguva yakawanda. Mutambo uyu unowanikwa mumutauro 100.
