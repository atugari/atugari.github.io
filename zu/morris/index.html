Iyini i-Mill (Nine Men's Morris)?

I-Mill (Nine Men's Morris) ingomunye wemidlalo yebhodi emidala kunazo zonke emhlabeni, edlalwa iminyaka engaphezu kuka-2,000. Abadlali ababili babelana ngebhodi elilodwa elinamaphuzu angama-24, axhunyiwe ngemigqa. Umdlali ngamunye unezingcezu eziyisishiyagalolunye — uhlangothi olulodwa lumnyama, olunye lumhlophe — kanti inhloso ilula: hlela izingcezu zakho ezintathu emgqeni ukuze uthathe izingcezu zomphikisi wakho, kuze kube yilapho umphikisi wakho esele ecishe engenalutho.

Lo mdlalo unezingxenye ezimbili. Okokuqala ubeka izingcezu zakho, esisodwa ngesikhathi, emaphuzwini angenalutho. Bese, uma zonke izingcezu sezisebhodini, uzihambisa emigqeni uye emaphuzwini angenalutho aseduze. Kuzwakala kulula, kodwa ukubeka ngakunye kubalulekile: ucezu olubekwe ngokunganaki ekuqaleni lungaba nzima ukuluvikela kamuva.

Ku-Pare Asobi ungadlala i-Mill mahhala esipheqululini sakho, ocingweni lwakho noma kukhompyutha yakho. Akukho lutho okufanele ululande futhi akukho akhawunti okufanele uyidale — vele uvule umdlalo bese uqala ukudlala.

Ungayidlala kanjani

  1. Okokuqala, beka izingcezu zakho. Shintshana ngokubeka ucezu olulodwa ephuzwini elingenalutho. Omnyama uhamba kuqala. Qhubeka kuze kube yilapho zonke izingcezu eziyisishiyagalolunye zohlangothi ngalunye sezisebhodini.
  2. Yenza i-mill ukuze uthathe ucezu. Uma izingcezu zakho ezintathu zihleleka emgqeni oxhunyiwe, usuyenzile i-mill, futhi ungakhipha ucezu olulodwa lomphikisi wakho ebhodini. Awukwazi ukuthatha ucezu oluphakathi kwe-mill — ngaphandle uma zonke izingcezu zomphikisi wakho ziphakathi kwe-mill.
  3. Bese uhambisa izingcezu zakho. Uma konke sekubekiwe, hambisa ucezu olulodwa ngesikhathi uye ephuzwini elingenalutho eliseduze emgqeni. I-mill eyenziwe ngokuhambisa nayo ikuvumela ukuba uthathe ucezu olulodwa lomphikisi wakho.
  4. Usele nezingcezu ezintathu? Ungandiza. Uma usele nezingcezu ezintathu kuphela, awusakhawulelwe emaphuzwini aseduze — ungandiza uye kunoma yiliphi iphuzu elingenalutho ebhodini.
  5. Ungawina kanjani. Uwina ngokunciphisa umphikisi wakho abe nezingcezu ezimbili, noma ngokumvimba aze angabi naso isinyathelo esisele. Uma isimo esifanayo sivela kathathu, noma uma kungekho mill eyenziwe ezinyathelweni ezingama-50, umdlalo uphela ngokulingana ngokuzenzakalelayo.

Izinhlobo ezimbili: i-Mill yakudala ne-Morabaraba

I-Mill yakudala (Nine Men's Morris) idlalwa ngezingcezu eziyisishiyagalolunye uhlangothi ngalunye, ebhodini lamaphuzu angama-24 elichazwe ngenhla.

I-Morabaraba inguhlobo lwezingcezu eziyi-12 lalo mdlalo. Umdlali ngamunye unezingcezu eziyi-12, futhi ibhodi lithola imigqa emine eyengeziwe ye-diagonal. Izingcezu zingahamba nge-diagonal futhi, kanti ezintathu emgqeni nge-diagonal nazo zenza i-mill. Idlalwa ngokushesha futhi ivuleke kakhulu kunomdlalo wakudala, futhi iklomelisa amehlo abukhali emigqeni ye-diagonal.

Zombili izinhlobo zitholakala emdlalweni ofanayo. Ungashintsha phakathi kwazo noma nini uma uqala umdlalo omusha.

Izindlela zokudlala

Dlala ku-inthanethi nabantu emhlabeni wonke. Qala umdlalo osheshayo bese uhlangana nomphikisi omusha ngokushesha. Umdlalo we-inthanethi unesilinganiso, izinga nezibukeli, ngezivinini ezintathu: esijwayelekile (imizuzwana engama-30 ngesinyathelo), esinensayo (imizuzu emi-2 ngesinyathelo) kanye neseposi (amahora angama-24 ngesinyathelo).

Dlala ngokumelene nekhompyutha. Kunamazinga amathathu ekhompyutha — alula, ajwayelekile, futhi aqinile — ukuze ukwazi ukufunda imithetho ngesivinini sakho bese ubhekana nomphikisi onzima kakhulu.

Dlala nabadlali ababili kudivayisi eyodwa. Yabelana ngocingo olulodwa noma isikrini esisodwa nomngane bese nishintshana ngokubeka nokuhambisa izingcezu, njengasebhodini langempela.

Lo mdlalo utholakala ngezilimi eziyi-100. Umdlalo ukhuluma ulimi lwakho, futhi ungawushintsha ezilungiselelweni noma nini.

Umdlalo we-inthanethi usutholakala manje

Manje ungadlala ku-inthanethi nabantu emhlabeni wonke. Qala umdlalo osheshayo bese uhlangana nomphikisi omusha ngokushesha.

Umdlalo we-inthanethi unezivinini ezintathu: esijwayelekile (imizuzwana engama-30 ngesinyathelo), esinensayo (imizuzu emi-2 ngesinyathelo) kanye neseposi (amahora angama-24 ngesinyathelo), unesilinganiso, izinga nezibukeli. Ungadlala futhi ngokumelene nekhompyutha noma nomngane kudivayisi efanayo.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe i-Mill (Nine Men's Morris) imahhala? Yebo. I-Mill ku-Pare Asobi imahhala ngokuphelele ukuyidlala, akukho ukubhalisa futhi akukho uhlelo lokusebenza okufanele ulufake.

Ngingakwazi ukudlala ocingweni lwami? Yebo. Umdlalo usebenza ezingcingweni, kumathebulethi nakumakhompyutha, ngqo esipheqululini sakho.

Iyini i-mill? I-mill izingcezu zakho ezintathu emgqeni emgqeni oxhunyiwe. Ukwenza i-mill kukuvumela ukuba ukhiphe ucezu olulodwa lomphikisi wakho ebhodini.

Iyini i-Morabaraba? I-Morabaraba inguhlobo lwezingcezu eziyi-12 lwe-Mill, olunemigqa emine eyengeziwe ye-diagonal ebhodini. Ukuhamba nge-diagonal nama-mill e-diagonal kuvunyelwe.

Ufuna eminye imidlalo? Bona yonke imidlalo eyisithupha yamahhala ku-ikhasi eliphezulu le-Pare Asobi ngesiZulu, okuhlanganisa i-Reversi (Othello), i-Sudoku (Nanpure), i-Crossword, i-Shirupure ne-Wado Kaichin.
