Iyini i-Reversi (Othello)?

I-Reversi, eyaziwa nangokuthi i-Othello, ingumdlalo webhodi wakudala wabadlali ababili. Umdlali oyedwa uthatha amadiski amnyama, omunye athathe amadiski amhlophe, kanti zombili izinhlangothi babelana ngebhodi elilodwa. Imithetho ithatha cishe umzuzu ukuyifunda, kodwa isinyathelo esisodwa singaguqula umdlalo wonke — yilokho okwenza i-Reversi ibe nzima ukuyibeka phansi.

Ibhodi elijwayelekile linamasikwele angu-8 ngo-8, kanti umdlalo uqala ngamadiski amane phakathi: amabili amnyama namabili amhlophe, abekwe nge-diagonal. Ukusuka lapho, abadlali bashintshana ngokubeka idiski elilodwa ngesikhathi, futhi wonke idiski abayibambayo iphenduka umbala wabo. Uma ibhodi ligcwele, noma uma kungekho mdlali onaso isinyathelo esisemthethweni esisele, umdlalo uyaphela kanti umdlali onamadiski amaningi ebhodini uyawina.

Ku-Pare Asobi ungadlala i-Reversi mahhala esipheqululini sakho, ocingweni lwakho noma kukhompyutha yakho. Akukho lutho okufanele ululande futhi akukho akhawunti okufanele uyidale — vele uvule umdlalo bese uqala ukudlala.

Ungayidlala kanjani

  1. Omnyama uhamba kuqala. Beka idiski esikweleni esingenalutho ukuze libambe idiski elilodwa noma amaningi omphikisi wakho emgqeni oqondile — nqamula, phansi, noma nge-diagonal — phakathi kwediski yakho entsha kanye nediski yombala wakho esivele isebhodini.
  2. Wonke idiski elibanjwe liphenduka umbala wakho. Isinyathelo esisodwa singapheqa umugqa wonke wamadiski ngesikhathi esisodwa, ngakho isinyathelo esisodwa esihle singashintsha amaphuzu kakhulu.
  3. Uma ungenaso isinyathelo esisemthethweni, uyadlula. Umphikisi wakho uhamba futhi. Umdlalo uyaqhubeka kuze kube yilapho ibhodi ligcwele noma kungekho mdlali ongahamba.
  4. Umdlali onamadiski amaningi uyawina. Amaphuzu okugcina ayinani nje lamadiski ombala ngamunye asele ebhodini.
  5. Amakhona ayizikwele eziphephe kunazo zonke. Uma usuthathe ikhona, angeke liphinde liphenduke. Izikwele eziseduze nekhona ziyingozi, ngoba zinganika lelo khona kumphikisi wakho.

Izindlela zokudlala

Dlala ku-inthanethi ngokumelene nabantu emhlabeni wonke. Qala umdlalo osheshayo bese uhlangana nomphikisi omusha ngokushesha. Imidlalo enesilinganiso isebenzisa isilinganiso, amazinga, izinga kanye neziqu, ukuze ukwazi ukubona isilinganiso sakho sikhuphuka uma uwina. Imidlalo yokuphumula ayinaso isilinganiso futhi inensayo, ukuze ukwazi ukujabulela umdlalo ngaphandle kokukhathazeka ngesilinganiso sakho.

Dlala ngokumelene nekhompyutha. Kunamazinga amahlanu ekhompyutha, kusukela kulula kuya kunamandla kakhulu. Qala ezingeni elilula ukuze ufunde imithetho, bese ukhuphuka izinga elilodwa ngesikhathi kuze kube yilapho izinga elinamandla kakhulu likunika inselelo yangempela.

Dlala nabadlali ababili kudivayisi eyodwa. Yabelana ngocingo olulodwa noma isikrini esisodwa nomngane bese nishintshana ngokubeka amadiski, njengasebhodini langempela.

Zama amabhodi anezimo ezingajwayelekile. Emidlalweni yekhompyutha nasemidlalweni yabadlali ababili kudivayisi eyodwa, ungakhetha isimo sebhodi: ibhodi elijwayelekile elingu-8 ngo-8, elibanzi, inhliziyo, idonathi, idayimane, noma isiphambano. Isimo ngasinye sishintsha ukuvula kanye nendlela umdlalo ovela ngayo.

Ngokushesha noma kancane — ukukhetha kwakho. Imidlalo ejwayelekile ikunika imizuzwana engama-20 ngesinyathelo ukuze udlale ngokushesha futhi ngokuphila. Uma ungathanda ukucabanga ngokucophelela, umdlalo wokuphumula ukunika isikhathi esiningi. Lo mdlalo utholakala ngezilimi eziyi-100.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe i-Reversi imahhala? Yebo. I-Reversi ku-Pare Asobi imahhala ngokuphelele ukuyidlala, akukho ukubhalisa futhi akukho uhlelo lokusebenza okufanele ulufake.

Ngingakwazi ukudlala ocingweni lwami? Yebo. Umdlalo usebenza ezingcingweni, kumathebulethi nakumakhompyutha, ngqo esipheqululini sakho.

Ingabe ngidinga i-akhawunti ukuze ngidlale ku-inthanethi? Cha. Ungaqala umdlalo osheshayo ku-inthanethi ngaphandle kokudala i-akhawunti.

Uyini umehluko phakathi kwe-Reversi ne-Othello? Ingumdlalo ofanayo. I-Othello igama elaziwa kakhulu le-Reversi, kanti womabili amagama asetshenziselwa umdlalo wokupheqa amadiski amnyama-namhlophe.

Ufuna eminye imidlalo? Bona yonke imidlalo eyisithupha yamahhala ku-ikhasi eliphezulu le-Pare Asobi ngesiZulu, okuhlanganisa i-Sudoku (Nanpure), i-Mill (Nine Men's Morris), i-Crossword, i-Shirupure ne-Wado Kaichin.
