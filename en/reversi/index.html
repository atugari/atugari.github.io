What is Reversi (Othello)?

Reversi, also known as Othello, is a classic board game for two players. One player takes the black discs, the other takes the white discs, and both sides share one board. The rules take about a minute to learn, but a single move can turn the whole game upside down — that is what makes Reversi so hard to put down.

The standard board is 8 by 8 squares, and the game starts with four discs in the middle: two black and two white, placed diagonally. From there, the players take turns placing one disc at a time, and every disc they trap flips to their color. When the board is full, or when neither player has a legal move left, the game ends and the player with more discs on the board wins.

How to play

  1. Black moves first. Place a disc on an empty square so that it traps one or more of your opponent's discs in a straight line — across, down, or diagonally — between your new disc and a disc of your own color already on the board.
  2. Every trapped disc flips to your color. A single move can flip a whole row of discs at once, so one good move can change the score by a lot.
  3. If you have no legal move, you pass. Your opponent moves again. Play continues until the board is full or neither player can move.
  4. The player with the most discs wins. The final score is simply the number of discs of each color left on the board.
  5. Corners are the safest squares. Once you take a corner, it can never be flipped. The squares right next to a corner are risky, because they can hand that corner to your opponent.
