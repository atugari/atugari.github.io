Mill (Nine Men's Morris) ni iki?

Mill (Nine Men's Morris) ni umwe mu mikino ya kera cyane y'ikibaho ku isi, wakinwe mu myaka irenga 2,000. Abakinnyi babiri basangira ikibaho kimwe gifite ingingo 24, zihujwe n'imirongo. Buri mukinnyi afite ibice icyenda — uruhande rumwe rwirabura, urundi rwera — kandi intego iroroshye: shyira ibice byawe bitatu ku murongo kugira ngo ufate ibice by'umukeba wawe, kugeza igihe umukeba wawe asigaranye hafi nta cyo.

Umukino ugizwe n'ibice bibiri. Mbere shyira ibice byawe, kimwe ku kindi, ku ngingo zidakoreshejwe. Hanyuma, iyo ibice byose bimaze kuba ku kibaho, ubinyeganyeze ukurikije imirongo ujya ku ngingo zegeranye zidakoreshejwe. Bisa nk'aho byoroshye, ariko buri gushyira ni ingenzi: igice gishyizweho utitondeye mu ntangiriro gishobora kugorana kugikingira nyuma.

Kuri Pare Asobi ushobora gukina Mill ku buntu muri mushakisha yawe, kuri telefoni yawe cyangwa kuri mudasobwa yawe. Nta cyo cyo gukuramo kandi nta konti yo gukora — fungura umukino gusa utangire gukina.

Uko gukina

  1. Mbere, shyira ibice byawe. Fata igihe cyo gushyira igice kimwe ku ngingo idakoreshejwe. Umukara wirabura ni we utangira. Komeza kugeza igihe ibice byose icyenda bya buri ruhande biri ku kibaho.
  2. Kora mill kugira ngo ufate igice. Iyo ibice byawe bitatu biri ku murongo ku murongo uhujwe, wakoze mill, kandi ushobora gukuramo igice kimwe cy'umukeba wawe ku kibaho. Ntushobora gufata igice kiri muri mill — keretse iyo ibice byose by'umukeba wawe biri muri mill.
  3. Hanyuma, nyeganyeza ibice byawe. Iyo byose bimaze gushyirwaho, nyeganyeza igice kimwe icyarimwe ujya ku ngingo yegeranye idakoreshejwe ukurikije umurongo. Mill ikozwe no kunyeganyeza nayo ikwemerera gufata igice kimwe cy'umukeba wawe.
  4. Usigaranye ibice bitatu gusa? Ushobora kuguruka. Iyo usigaranye ibice bitatu gusa, ntugikoreshwa n'ingingo zegeranye gusa — ushobora kuguruka ujya ku ngingo iyo ari yo yose idakoreshejwe ku kibaho.
  5. Uko gutsinda. Utsinda mu kugabanya umukeba wawe akagera ku bice bibiri, cyangwa mu kumubuza kugira ngo adakora. Niba aho hantu hamwe hagarutse inshuro eshatu, cyangwa niba nta mill ikozwe mu ntera 50, umukino urangira ungana ubwawo.

Ubwoko bubiri: Mill ya kera na Morabaraba

Mill ya kera (Nine Men's Morris) ikinwa n'ibice icyenda kuri buri ruhande, ku kibaho gifite ingingo 24 cyasobanuwe haruguru.

Morabaraba ni ubwoko bw'ibice 12 bw'umukino. Buri mukinnyi afite ibice cumi na bibiri, kandi ikibaho kibona imirongo ine y'inyongera ihengamye. Ibice bishobora no kunyeganyezwa mu buryo buhengamye, kandi bitatu ku murongo mu buryo buhengamye nabyo bikora mill. Ukina vuba kandi ukinguye kurusha umukino wa kera, kandi uhemba amaso akaze ku mirongo ihengamye.

Ubwoko bwombi buboneka mu mukino umwe. Ushobora guhindura hagati yabyo igihe cyose utangiye umukino mushya.

Uburyo bwo gukina

Kina ku murongo urwanya abantu hirya no hino ku isi. Tangira umukino wihuse uhure umukeba mushya ako kanya. Umukino wo ku murongo ufite amanota, urutonde, n'abarebera, ufite umuvuduko itatu: usanzwe (amasegonda 30 kuri buri ntera), buhoro (iminota 2 kuri buri ntera) n'inyandiko (amasaha 24 kuri buri ntera).

Kina urwanya mudasobwa. Hari urwego eshatu rwa mudasobwa — rworoshye, rusanzwe, n'rukomeye — kugira ngo wige amategeko ku muvuduko wawe bwite hanyuma uhangane n'umukeba ukomeye.

Kina n'abakinnyi babiri ku gikoresho kimwe. Sangira telefoni imwe cyangwa igisobanuro kimwe n'inshuti maze mufate igihe cyo gushyira no kunyeganyeza ibice, nk'ikibaho nyacyo.

Umukino uboneka mu ndimi 100. Umukino uvuga ururimi rwawe, kandi ushobora kuruhindura uhereye ku igenamiterere igihe cyose.

Gukina ku murongo kuboneka ubu

Ubu, ushobora gukina ku murongo urwanya abantu hirya no hino ku isi. Tangira umukino wihuse uhure umukeba mushya ako kanya.

Umukino wo ku murongo ufite umuvuduko itatu: usanzwe (amasegonda 30 kuri buri ntera), buhoro (iminota 2 kuri buri ntera) n'inyandiko (amasaha 24 kuri buri ntera), ufite amanota, urutonde, n'abarebera. Ushobora no gukina urwanya mudasobwa cyangwa n'inshuti ku gikoresho kimwe.

Ibibazo bikunze kubazwa

Mill (Nine Men's Morris) ni ubuntu? Yego. Mill kuri Pare Asobi ni ubuntu rwose gukina, nta kwiyandikisha kandi nta porogaramu yo gushyiraho.

Nshobora gukina kuri telefoni yanjye? Yego. Umukino ukora kuri telefoni, ibinini, na mudasobwa, mu buryo butaziguye muri mushakisha yawe.

Mill ni iki? Mill ni ibice byawe bitatu ku murongo ku murongo uhujwe. Gukora mill bikwemerera gukuramo igice kimwe cy'umukeba wawe ku kibaho.

Morabaraba ni iki? Morabaraba ni ubwoko bw'ibice 12 bwa Mill, bufite imirongo ine y'inyongera ihengamye ku kibaho. Intera zihengamye na mill zihengamye ziremewe.
